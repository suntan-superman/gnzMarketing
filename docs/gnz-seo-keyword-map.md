## Audience separation

GNZ should speak to businesses, organizations, owners, investors, buyers, and strategic partners. SellMyKernHome should remain focused on the homeowner/seller audience and its seller-intake journey. Do not copy seller-property pages, homeowner claims, or transaction language onto GNZ pages; cross-site links should be descriptive and purposeful.

## Content guidance

- Use terms such as acquisitions, due diligence, investor relations, dispositions, and strategic partnerships only where the page accurately describes GNZ's role.
- Avoid implying licensed brokerage activity, guaranteed returns, securities offerings, managed funds, or investment advice.
- Add location-specific terms only after GNZ confirms its actual service area and approved local claims.
- Build future Insights around approved, original GNZ perspectives rather than automatically republishing legacy Hub summaries.
